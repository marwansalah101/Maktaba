Our Story

More than a bookstore

"Founded in 2024, Maktaba — meaning 'library' in Arabic — was born from a simple belief : that the right book, at the right moment, can change everything. We see ourselves as more than just an online bookstore; we are a digital sanctuary for the modern bibliophile. At Maktaba, we believe every book is a doorway to a new world. Our team hand-picks every edition, focusing on quality, cultural significance, and the power of the written word. Whether you are a lifelong scholar or a curious newcomer, Our mission is to preserve the art of deep reading in a fast-paced age, connecting readers with stories that linger long after the final page is turned. From the weathered wisdom of timeless classics to the bold voices of the latest bestsellers, we provide a curated space to discover, purchase, and truly immerse yourself in the world of literature. Maktaba is your partner in building a personal library that reflects who you are & who you want to become."
