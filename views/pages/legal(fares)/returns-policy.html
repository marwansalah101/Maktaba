Return Policy

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Return Policy

1. Loan Periods

Borrowing times vary depending on the type of material:

  • Standard Books: 21 days (3 weeks).
  • New Releases: 14 days (2 weeks).
  • Magazines & Periodicals: 7 days (1 week).
  • Digital Media (DVDs/Audiobooks): 14 days (2 weeks).

2. Renewing Materials

If you need more time with an item, you may renew it under the following conditions:

  • Items can be renewed up to two times if no other member has placed a "Hold" on them.
  • Renewals can be done online through your account, via our mobile app, or in person at the front desk.

3. How to Return Items

You can return physical materials using the following methods:

  • Internal Drop Box: Located at the main circulation desk during library hours.
  • 24/7 External Chute: Located at the north entrance of the building for after-hours returns.
  • Inter-Library Return: Items borrowed from partner libraries may be returned here, but please allow 3 days for processing.

4. Overdue and Lost Items

We are a Fine-Free Library for most items, meaning we do not charge daily late fees. However:

  • Lost Status: Items 30 days past the due date are marked "Lost."
  • Replacement Fees: If an item is lost or returned with significant damage (water damage, missing pages), the user will be charged the retail replacement cost plus a $5 processing fee.
  • Account Suspension: Accounts with "Lost" items totaling more than $20 will be temporarily suspended from borrowing until the items are returned or paid for.

5. Damaged Items

Please do not attempt to repair library materials yourself with tape or glue. Notify a librarian upon return so our professional conservation team can handle the repairs.
